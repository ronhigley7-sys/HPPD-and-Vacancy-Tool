The rows pinned above the dates track the whole timeline: vacancy % without travelers, vacancy % with travelers (both RN-only), HPPD (RN + CA + LPN combined), Target HPPD, and ADC. Cells show each person's FTE once active (e.g. 0.9), ORI during a traveler's first 7 days (not counted in RN totals), DBL for anyone on disability leave (also excluded), and blank before start / after end. Travelers past their end date move to Archived automatically. For any week with logged daily data, HPPD and ADC use that actual data; for weeks without it, ADC is forecast from the trailing ~4 weeks of logged days (or the fallback setting if there's no history yet) and HPPD is estimated from FTE using that forecast (1.0 FTE = 40 hrs/week) — an asterisk (*) marks forecast/estimated values.
